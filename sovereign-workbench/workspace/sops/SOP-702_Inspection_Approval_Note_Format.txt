STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP-702)
GOVERNMENT & DEFENCE MANUFACTURING / REFINERY PLANT DIVISION
SUBJECT: GUIDELINES FOR DRAFTING INSPECTION APPROVAL NOTES & COMPLIANCE SIGN-OFFS

1. PURPOSE & APPLICABILITY
1.1 This SOP specifies the mandatory format and required sections for all Plant Equipment Inspection Approval Notes across Refineries, Defence Units, and PSU Power Plants.
1.2 Any equipment (Valves, Pressure Vessels, Turbines, Pipelines) undergoing quarterly or annual inspection must be documented using an official Approval Note prior to recommissioning.

2. MANDATORY APPROVAL NOTE SECTIONS
Every generated Approval Note must contain the following 4 sections:
Section I: Header & Facility Metadata (Ref Number, Facility Unit Name, Inspection Date).
Section II: Key Inspection Findings (Detailed list of physical observations, pressure tests, gauge readings, and defect statuses).
Section III: Grounding & SOP Reference (Explicit citation of applicable SOP clauses and compliance threshold validations).
Section IV: Recommendation & Sign-off Block (Clear recommendation for 6 to 12-month operational authorization, signed by the Chief Inspection Officer).

3. COMPLIANCE & SAFETY THRESHOLDS
3.1 Pressure Vessels & Valves: Hydrostatic pressure tests must maintain minimum 150 PSI for at least 30 minutes without pressure drop exceeding 0.5%.
3.2 Surface Corrosion: Surface pitting/wear depth must not exceed 0.8mm. Wear exceeding 1.2mm requires mandatory component replacement before sign-off.
3.3 Recommissioning Clearance: Operational approval may be granted for a maximum duration of 12 months, subject to annual re-inspection.

4. REJECTION & ESCALATION PROTOCOL
4.1 If pressure drop during testing exceeds 1.0% or surface wear exceeds 1.2mm, the equipment must be marked 'REJECTED / MAINTENANCE REQUIRED'.
4.2 Escalation notes must be submitted to the General Manager (Safety & Operations) within 24 hours of inspection failure.
